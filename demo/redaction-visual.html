Everything sensitive is caught before the boundary.

A browser agent needs to see the page to help you fill it in. This one detects faces, signatures, and identifiers on your device and redacts them, so the reasoning server receives a map of the page — never your identity.

Raw PII never crosses. Detect → fuse → decide → redact, all in the browser.

Four stages, all on-device

The pipeline

01

Detect

DOM semantics, checksum-validated text scan, and a WebGPU/CPU vision detector for faces & signatures.

02

Fuse

Union-biased merge of the three signals (noisy-OR, IoU). Two weak hints for one region become one strong one.

03

Decide

Fail-closed policy: faces & signatures → blackout, text → tokenize. When uncertain, redact.

04

Redact

Compose the masked screenshot, tokenize labels, draw Set-of-Marks, and emit an auditable receipt.
